## What Is Inpaint

Inpaint is a cleanup helper for ordinary photos. Inpaint object removal is the main gesture: mark what should leave, then let the nearby pixels suggest a fill. Inpaint background fill is that suggestion, whether the area is a wall, a path, or a bit of sky. It suits a home album, a school poster, or a small project where one distraction ruins an otherwise good frame.

Inpaint watermark clean and Inpaint text erase are narrower jobs. Use them on pictures you have the right to edit, such as your own travel photo or a scan of a print you own. Inpaint photo heal is for specks and scratches rather than for rebuilding a whole scene. None of these steps needs a large retouch desk. A few careful marks are the whole method.

Work on a copy. Try Inpaint object removal on one obvious item, then judge Inpaint background fill at full size. Use Inpaint text erase or Inpaint watermark clean only when the words are yours to change. Finish with Inpaint photo heal for the dust you still see. If the fill looks wrong, undo and mark a smaller area.
